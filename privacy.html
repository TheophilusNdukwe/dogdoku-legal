Dogdoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Dogdoku (“the app”) is a casual Sudoku game published by Theophilus Ndukwe (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app may collect and how it is used. It applies to the iOS and Android versions of Dogdoku.

No account, local saves only

Dogdoku does not have an account system. You do not create a username or password to play. Game progress, coin balance, daily streak, Dog Park timers, settings (including sound and music), hint and booster counts, and whether you have purchased Remove Ads are saved locally on your device. This save data stays on the device unless you back it up with your platform (for example iCloud or Google backup) according to that platform’s rules.

Purchases

Optional coin packs and the Remove Ads product are processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit / Unity IAP) or by Google through Google Play billing (Unity IAP). We do not collect or store your credit-card number, security code, or full account password. The stores tell the app whether a product was purchased or restored so we can grant coins or Remove Ads. Receipt validation, if enabled in a later version, may send a purchase receipt to a server we operate or to a validation service solely to confirm the purchase is genuine.

Advertising

If you have not purchased Remove Ads, the app shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads through AppLovin MAX and the advertising networks it works with. The AppLovin SDK and those partners may collect the following, linked to your device:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Google advertising ID on Android) and the vendor or app-set ID.
  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, language, carrier, screen size, and IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate (city-level) location.
  • Usage data: app sessions and how you interact with ads, such as impressions, clicks, and rewarded ad completions.
  • Diagnostics: crash, error, and performance data from the ad SDK, used to keep ads working.

This data is used to serve, measure, cap, and personalize ads, to prevent ad fraud, and to fix ad problems. On iOS, the app asks your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising ID is used for tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” you still get ads, but they are not personalized using the IDFA. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. We do not sell your personal information. See AppLovin’s privacy policy for how AppLovin handles this data. Purchasing Remove Ads turns off banner and interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain optional. Removing ads does not delete data ad partners have already collected.

Analytics and diagnostics

Dogdoku itself does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. The only usage and diagnostic data collected comes from the AppLovin advertising SDK described above, plus anonymous sales, install, and crash reports that Apple and Google provide to developers through their stores. If we add an analytics or crash tool later, we will update this policy and name the provider.

Children

Dogdoku is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app or an ad partner, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Your choices

  • Turn sound and music off in Settings.
  • Restore purchases from Settings or the Shop (required on iOS).
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop banner and interstitial ads in the app.
  • On iOS, allow or deny tracking from the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Reset or delete local progress from the app or by uninstalling it (this does not cancel a store purchase).
  • Use your device settings to reset or limit the advertising ID.

Data retention and international transfer

Local save data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Ad and store partners may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States, under their own policies. We keep any support email you send only as long as needed to respond and keep a record of the request.

Contact

For privacy questions, access, or deletion requests, email theondukwe@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change (for example if we add analytics or server-side receipt checks). The “Last updated” date at the top will change, and a material change will be described here.

This policy describes current practices for Dogdoku. It is not legal advice.
